Our Concept

Designed for flavor explorers, Lil Honey is an intimate, boutique specialty coffee spot located in Ridgewood. Born as a thoughtful sister extension to the beloved Honey Day Cafe in nearby Glen Rock, we bridge quick neighborhood convenience with unique culinary execution.

Every pour, cold foam layer, and morning pastry pairing is built specifically to add a sweet, highly aesthetic moment to your daily routine.
